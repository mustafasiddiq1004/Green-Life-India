GOVERNMENT SUBSIDY

Make solar more affordable with central & state support.

The Government of India offers substantial subsidies for rooftop solar installations under the PM Surya Ghar scheme, making clean energy accessible for more households.

  • ✓ Up to 40% subsidy for systems up to 3 kW
  • ✓ 20% subsidy for systems up to 10 kW
  • ✓ Net metering benefits
  • ✓ Accelerated depreciation for businesses
*Subsidy amounts vary by state and system size. Contact us for a detailed assessment.
SYSTEM TYPES

On-Grid, Off-Grid or Hybrid?

Choose the solar system that fits your needs — whether you want to stay connected to the grid, go fully independent, or have the best of both worlds.

Grid-Connected ⚡

On-Grid Solar

Connected to the utility grid. Excess power is exported and you earn credits via net metering.

  • Lower electricity bills
  • No battery required
  • Best for urban areas
Standalone 🔋

Off-Grid Solar

Independent system with battery storage. Ideal for remote areas with unreliable or no grid access.

  • 100% energy independence
  • Battery backup included
  • Works during power cuts
Flexible 🔄

Hybrid Solar

Combines on-grid and off-grid features with battery storage, giving you flexibility and backup.

  • Grid + battery backup
  • Export excess power
  • Best for power-cut areas
